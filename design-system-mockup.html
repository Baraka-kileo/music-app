One design system for the whole app

A mockup to agree the look before it is built. The top row is the rule set; the three phones show the profile menu, Settings and a Settings page drawn with only those rules.

The rules

Colour
Dark canvas, three glass levels, one gradient ("Ember"), one red. No colour per section: the gradient marks only what is on, selected or the main action.
Type
Display 30 accent
Title 20
Row title 15
Detail 13
One family in four sizes. The italic serif is kept for big headlines only.
Shape and spacing
Five corner sizes (tile 10, control 16, card 24, sheet 32, pill) instead of today's 14. Spacing in steps of 4.
Icons
Phosphor icons: one modern family. Outline by default, the filled twin when something is active (liked, current tab, playing).
Controls
LowHigh
Primary
Secondary
One switch, one picker, two buttons, used everywhere.
Touch feedback
Tickswitch, picker, tab change, slider stepsClickplay, pause, skip, main buttonsPoplike, add to playlist, download doneThudlong-press menu, drag to reorderNonescrolling, plain navigation, typing
Four vibrations with fixed meanings, plus a press-shrink on everything tappable. One switch in Settings turns them off.
